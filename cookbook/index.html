Lots of advantages!

Instead of having one finger in the recipe text, one on the list of ingredients, and two arms at the stove, you see everything clearly laid out for you, so you can use both hands for cooking.

Efficient

  • See the ingredients when they are used (rather than as a list at the beginning nowhere near their use)
  • See which pots, implements, etc. to use

Logical

  • See what can be done in parallel
  • See how the various intermediate products get merged together
  • Clear, unambiguous notation (like critical-path diagrams, formulae for weights, cooking times, etc.)

Intuitive

  • Recognise the recipes immediately while thumbing through (unlike text recipes which all look the same)
  • Hints and tests to ensure success (like the famous Hoi-Sin-Sauce comparison)

About the book

The recipes have been gleaned from various sources (mainly friends, restaurants and other - inferior - cookbooks), then optimised to maximise pleasure whilst stripping away any unnecessary effort (compare our Peking Duck recipe to the original!). Finally all have been tested until they are bug-free. We use them every day.
