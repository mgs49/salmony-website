In no particular order and also not including films of theatre productions: Faust, Anyone for Dennis, Wim Wender’s Film about Pina Bausch etc…

- Funny
  - The Big Lebowski – Jeff Bridges, John Goodman (Joel Coen)
  - Animations: Toy Story 2, Shrek 2, Monsters vs Aliens 3D, Rapunzel
  - In Bruges
  - The Rocky Horror Picture Show – Tim Curry (Jim Sharman)
  - Four Weddings and a Funeral – witty, romantic, emotional
- Future
  - Looper  – clever, different plot, “Go to China, I’ve sen the future”
  - The Martian – exciting, science, different plot, …
  - Her
  - The Matrix (the first one, obvs)
  - Metropolis – (Fritz Lang)
  - Ghost in the Shell (Scarlett Johansson in a future Tokyo, the Anime is great too (not the many later clones) … better than the original cyberpunk Manga) … indeed all these kind of future films e.g. Alita, Ready Player One, Ender’s Game, Lucy, Mortal Engines, I Robot, etc
- Strange
  - One Flew over the Cuckoo’s Nest – Jack Nicholson (Milos Forman)
  - Das Fest – Dogma
  - Harold & Maude – (Hal Ashby)
  - eXistenZ – (David Cronenberg) – try to keep track of the levels …
- Gripping
  - Talk to her – (Almodovar)
  - Dogville – Nicole Kidman (Lars von Trier)
  - Leon – Jean Reno, Natalie Portman + plant (Luc Besson)
  - Silence of the Lambs – Jodie Foster
  - The Assassin – Bridget Fonda version of Nikita
- Music/Artistic/Beautiful
  - Meeting Venus – Glenn Close (Istvan Szabo)
  - Three Colours: Blue (not the other colours…), fab music, bible text – (Kieslowski)
  - Interiors – the only good Woody Allen
  - Babette’s Feast
