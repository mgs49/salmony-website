Why I Like Working With Technology

Because nothing has changed the world as much

  • Not art – unfortunately
  • Not politics – luckily
  • Not human nature – no chance …

Instead it has been the likes of

The discovery of fire, the club, flint tools, agriculture, the longbow, motors, gunpowder, the atom bomb,  anesthetics, disinfectant, TV, computers, and genetics i.e. technologies which have fundamentally changed the way we live.

And because technology is fun.

Another candidate that changes the world and the way one lives in is money (and I like that too).
