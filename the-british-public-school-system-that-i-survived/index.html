King's School, Canterbury

Not everyone enjoyed King's. Somerset Maugham was so unhappy there that at 16 he refused to go back, and put it all into Of Human Bondage. John Lloyd (Not the Nine O'Clock News, Blackadder, QI) called winning his scholarship there the worst thing that ever happened to him.

I loved it - except for Goddard, our housemaster, who later became Lord Mayor of Oxford.
